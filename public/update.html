Update Simple Reader

Checking for a new version…

Simple Reader runs from the folder you added with Load unpacked. Update now downloads the new release from GitHub, checks it, writes it into that folder, and restarts the extension. Your settings and saved reading positions are kept.

The first time, the browser asks you to choose a folder. Choose the Simple Reader folder you loaded with Load unpacked: the one with manifest.json directly inside it (open Details for Simple Reader on the extensions page and look under "Loaded from"). Then allow the extension to edit files there. Later updates go straight through, or ask you to confirm once.
